Insurance Glossary & Terms Center

Educational reference guide for health insurance and ML terms.

Annual Premium

The total lump-sum amount paid per year to maintain health insurance coverage.

Co-payment (Co-pay)

A fixed percentage of medical bills paid out-of-pocket by the policyholder.

Deductible

The threshold amount you must pay before your insurance policy begins contributing.
